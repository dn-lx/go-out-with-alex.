# Go Out with Alex — improved invitation journey

1. **Fullscreen invitation.** Bunny introduces a tiny surprise. On browsers that do not support fullscreen, the visitor can maximize the screen and continue.
2. **Private invitation password.** A per-recipient access code reveals her name and the question. The temporary test code is shared only outside the public source files.
3. **The question.** "[Guest name], would you like to go out with Alex?" Yes 🥰✅ and No 😏❌ are large emoji buttons. No dodges three times with cheeky expressions, then gives up. A declining answer is always respected.
4. **Choose an activity.** Coffee or tea, a walk, a bite to eat, a sweet treat, or a little surprise.
5. **A useful follow-up specific to the activity:**
   - Coffee → cosy café, takeaway-and-stroll, tea/another drink, or decide together.
   - Walk → park, city, water, or decide together.
   - Food → Italian, Asian, casual/street food, or decide together.
   - Sweet treat → bakery, ice cream, waffles, or decide together.
   - Surprise → calm/cozy, playful, outdoors, or Alex chooses.
6. **Preferred meeting area.** Near her, near Alex, halfway, or choose a place together.
7. **Day and time from a real interactive calendar.** Browse the current and next three months, select an available future/today day, and then choose Morning, Afternoon, Evening or Flexible. Alternatively, choose "Pick a day together later".
8. **Summary.** Present the activity, specific preference, meeting area, calendar day and time. All choices remain editable by going back.
9. **Send voluntarily.** Only after choosing "Send my choices to Alex" does the Netlify Form store the result; she can also share or copy. No answer is automatically sent.
10. **Bunny ending.** Celebratory or graceful decline, with distinct expressions and animation.

## Visual direction

The website contains an animated gray-and-white original cartoon-rabbit illustration with long ears, buck teeth, whiskers, a carrot and envelope. Animated eye glances, blinking, head movement, ears, mouth, hand and carrot bring it to life. This is **not an official licensed Bugs Bunny asset**; replace it only if rights to official media are secured. A separately generated fan-art GIF can be used for concept exploration.

No "date" terminology appears in the invitation UI: it asks to go out and choose an activity, a day and a time.
