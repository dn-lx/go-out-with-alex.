# Tiny Bunny Mission — invitation wording

1. **Fullscreen:** “A tiny surprise awaits. Bunny has one little request first. Make this screen as big as possible so the magic feels extra special.”
2. **Password:** “Secret bunny entrance. This little surprise was made just for you. Enter your invitation password to open it.”
3. **Question:** “[Guest Name], would you like to go out with Alex?” The Yes face gets heart eyes; the No face pulls a cheeky expression and dodges briefly twice, then remains selectable.
4. **Something fun:** Coffee and conversation; a walk and exploring; something sweet; or surprise me.
5. **Preferred vibe:** Relaxed; playful; adventurous; or whatever feels natural.
6. **Timing:** This weekend; next week; or let's figure it out together.
7. **Happy ending:** Bunny celebrates and shows the selected preferences. Sending to Alex is optional.
8. **Decline:** “Absolutely no worries. Thank you for answering. You're lovely, and this bunny wishes you a wonderful day.”
